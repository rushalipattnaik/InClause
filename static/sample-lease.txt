SAMPLE RESIDENTIAL LEASE

1. This sample Lease Agreement, which is fictional and only for trying out InClause, is made on 1 March 2026 between Asha Verma (the Owner) and Ravi Kulkarni (the Tenant) for the flat described in Schedule A.

2. The lease runs for eleven months from 1 March 2026 and renews automatically for further periods of eleven months unless the Tenant gives notice at least sixty days before the end of a period.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 22,000 on or before the 5th day of each month.

4. If rent is not paid by the due date, the Tenant shall pay a penalty of Rs. 500 for each day of delay.

5. The Owner may revise the rent at any time at its sole discretion without prior notice to the Tenant.

6. The Tenant has paid a security deposit of Rs. 66,000 which is non-refundable and may be retained by the Owner for any reason.

7. The Tenant shall pay electricity and water charges as per the actual meter readings.

8. The Owner shall pay all property taxes and the annual fee of the housing society.

9. The Tenant shall not sublet, assign or part with possession of the flat to any person without the prior written consent of the Owner.

10. Minor day-to-day repairs shall be done by the Tenant at his own cost, while structural repairs shall be carried out by the Owner.

11. The Owner or her agents may enter the flat at any time, with or without notice, for any purpose.

12. The Tenant shall keep the flat in good condition and return it in the same condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

13. Either party may terminate this lease by giving two months written notice to the other party.

14. If the Tenant vacates before the end of the lease period, the Tenant shall forfeit the entire security deposit and shall pay rent for the remaining months.

15. If the Tenant does not vacate on the last day of the lease, the Tenant shall pay damages at three times the monthly rent for every month of overstay.

16. Any dispute shall be settled by arbitration in Pune before a sole arbitrator appointed by the Owner, whose decision shall be final and binding.

17. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and the courts at Pune shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

18. The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect its meaning.

19. Notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses written above.
